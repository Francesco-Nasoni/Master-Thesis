    \chapter{Theoretical Background}

    This chapter is devoted to the review of some of the theoretical background that explain the physical principles behind the main topics treated in this thesis work.

    The first part of the chapter focusses on traditional step index optical fiber, with the aim to explain the physics of mode confinement and obtain equations that describe the electromagnetic field behavior in the fibers, this lead with the appropriate approximations to the $LP$ modes.

    The second part of the chapter focuses, instead, on the physical explanation of how an optical dipole trap can trap atoms, with the aim of obtaining a form for the trapping potential for both ideal two-level and multi level atoms.
